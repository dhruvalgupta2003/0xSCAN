### What does not count

Do not pad this section — it is more damaging than leaving it thin. The following do not count as international exposure:

- "Cross-cultural collaboration" as a keyword with no incident behind it
- Leisure or family travel presented as business exposure
- Working for a company that has international operations when your own work never touched them
- Serving customers across Indian states reframed as "cross-cultural"
- Using international software, attending webinars, or completing an online course from a foreign university
- Stretching the MIT collaboration into "international project leadership"

If the hunt comes up empty, we keep the International line short and factual and carry the argument in your essays as motivation rather than credential: you have built commercially in a domestic market and now need cross-border capability you cannot acquire where you are. Adcoms accept that readily. They do not accept an inflated resume.

## Things you can start now that will appear on this application

**1. Book the test.** Eight to twelve weeks of preparation. Nothing else on this page comes close in value.

**2. Run a quantified project at Lifomed and report the result.** You run the commercial function of a company you can actually direct, so you can generate legitimate resume facts by doing real work. Options that produce a reportable number in two to four months:

- Enter a new district or state; report revenue and coverage gained
- Onboard a defined number of new distributors; report coverage growth
- Renegotiate packaging or API sourcing; report the margin improvement
- Digitise order-taking; report the reduction in order-cycle time
- Reprice the top SKUs; report the margin impact
- **Initiate and evaluate an export enquiry to Nepal, Bangladesh or a Gulf market** — the standout option, because it is the only item here that fixes the international gap with a genuine fact rather than better wording

**3. Start a language.** Alliance Française, Instituto Cervantes or Goethe-Institut. A2 is achievable in about three months part-time and maps directly onto INSEAD's third-language requirement.

**4. Take a committee role with a local chemists' or pharmaceutical distributors' association.** Realistically obtainable, adjacent to your actual work, and gives you a title with a constituency behind it — far more credible than a volunteering stint started the month before the deadline.

**5. Learn Power BI or Excel modelling and build something real** — a distributor performance dashboard at Lifomed. The certificate alone is worth nothing; the artefact is worth a bullet.

### Not worth your time

LinkedIn Learning badges, generic Coursera and edX certificates, "digital marketing" certifications, one-day webinars, MOOCs without assessment, paid memberships with no role attached, and short volunteering stints begun just before the deadline — which read as exactly what they are.

The one exception worth considering is a short export-import or international trade certificate from a recognised body such as an IIFT short programme, FIEO or a DGFT-linked course, because it maps onto the stated post-MBA goal. Even that is worth about a point, and it is not a substitute for the test.

## Where this profile realistically lands

Average tenure at these schools is five to six years against your three. With everything on this worksheet completed, roughly 10–20%. **Tier B — IE Business School, ESADE, Cambridge Judge, Rotterdam School of Management, Imperial.** This is the target tier. Roughly 40–60% with a 645+ Focus score and full disclosure, with real scholarship potential.

**Tier C — Manchester, Warwick, Cranfield, Durham, Bayes, EDHEC, Grenoble, EADA, Hult.** Strong candidate and scholarship-competitive with the additions above.

**Two alternatives worth considering rather than dismissing.** A one-year specialised MSc in International Business or Global Management — RSM's CEMS track, ESCP, Grenoble, Hult's MIB — often delivers better return than a stretched MBA at three years of experience, and CEMS in particular supplies exactly the international network you currently lack. Or defer one to two cycles and apply with five years of experience and a documented cross-border initiative behind you; on expected value that is the strongest path to Tier A and it costs only time.
